ABOUT BIHAR

Journey Through History & Culture

Bihar is one of India's most historically and culturally significant states. It has been an important center of Buddhism, Jainism, education and ancient civilization.

From the ancient ruins of Nalanda to the peaceful temples of Bodh Gaya, Bihar offers a unique travel experience.
